# 2026-07-06

- Watched: Mental models for building products people love ft. Stewart Butterfield (Lenny's Podcast) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kLe-zy5r0Mk
  Key takeaways:
  - "We don't sell saddles here" — sell the transformation/outcome, not the tool or features
  - Divine discontent: user expectations always rise; stay permanently dissatisfied, polish past good-enough
  - Watch for "hyper-realistic work-like activities" — busywork that mimics progress but adds no value
  - Quitting well is a skill — both Slack and Flickr came from killing failed games and extracting the one part that worked
  - The owner's delusion: founders overvalue their own product; users don't think about it like you do
